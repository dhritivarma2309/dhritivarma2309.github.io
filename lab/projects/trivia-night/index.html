Trivia Night

A Jeopardy-style game for hosting trivia with friends and family over a video call. Everyone buzzes from their own phone. The questions are drafted by me and Claude.

Claude, Firebase, Cloudflare

The idea

Many of my friends and family live far away. I wanted to host trivia nights that bring everyone together, let us compete a little and, most importantly, share a laugh or two.

The product

A Jeopardy-style game for hosting trivia over video calls with friends and family. It has three parts across two personas.

Quiz Master

Build a Board

Write six categories of five questions by hand, or give Claude a theme and edit what it drafts.

Quiz Master

Host a Game

Open a room, share the code on the call, then pick tiles, judge answers and keep score.

My learnings

Tools

Firebase
A real-time database that syncs the board, buzzes and scores across every phone, plus Google sign-in for my board library.
Cloudflare
A small server that keeps the Claude API key out of the browser and lets only me use AI drafting.
Claude
My engineering partner for writing the code and tests, and the model that drafts the trivia questions.
